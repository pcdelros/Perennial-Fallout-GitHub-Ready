Clint — Vocals
A poet turned lyricist, Clint brings raw emotion, conviction, and authenticity to every song. His lyrics challenge the status quo while encouraging listeners to think for themselves and seek the truth.

Pete — Lead Guitar
With many years of playing experience, Pete delivers powerful solos, memorable hooks, and dynamic songwriting. His goal is simple: inspire, uplift, and connect with people through music.

Chad — Rhythm Guitar
The driving force behind the band's rhythm section, Chad brings energy, precision, and a passion for creating music that resonates on a deeper level.

Donnie — Drums
Known for his hard-hitting style and modern metal influence, Donnie provides the relentless groove and explosive energy that powers the band's live performances.

Odin — Bass
Delivering thunderous low end and bone-rattling riffs, Odin draws inspiration from artists ranging from Tool and Mudvayne to classic hard rock and modern metal.
